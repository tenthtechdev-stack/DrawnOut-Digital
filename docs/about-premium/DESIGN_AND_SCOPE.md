An asymmetric founder hero leads into numbered origin, founders, manifesto, process, studio difference, capabilities and invitation chapters. Layouts change scale and contrast instead of repeating cards.

The two external agency reference images mentioned in the brief were not included in this submission or located among the supplied attachment files. Their described qualities guide the design; no competitor assets are copied or claimed to have been inspected.

## Motion

One interruptible hero entrance establishes portrait, plaque, localized warm atmosphere, then headline and copy in approximately 3–4 seconds. The page uses native scrolling. Process artwork follows per-scene scroll progress directly in both directions, without delayed scrubbing or pinning. No-JavaScript and accessibility preferences render complete static content.

## Review boundary

Checkpoints preserve implementation early, followed by real desktop/mobile recordings, five-width screenshots, static fallbacks, visual inspection and measured tests. No automatic merge, deployment or Netlify mirror push.
