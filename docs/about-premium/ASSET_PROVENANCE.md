# About redesign — assets and representation

The new [owner brief](IMPLEMENTATION_BRIEF.md) explicitly authorizes reusing the
existing illuminated plaque appearance from `SignatureArtwork.astro`. It
supersedes the preceding milestone's requirement to withhold that appearance
until editable 3D geometry is supplied.

## Founders

The hero and founders section use the previously approved expressive charcoal
and orange sketch, `src/assets/about/founders-sketch-approved.png`, through the
existing approved portrait data. No new faces or identities are generated.
Neither founder's name is assigned to a position in the illustration. The
original personal photograph and rejected candidates remain private and are not
added to this branch.

## Branded plaque

`src/assets/signature/emblem-plaque.png` is the exact existing separated raster
asset used by the homepage and signature footer. Its illuminated glass-like
appearance comes from the supplied reference pixels. About uses a layered
presentation of that approved object, with responsive positioning and restrained
motion. It is **not an editable 3D model, newly ray-traced emblem, independently
relit object or recovered geometric source**.

The source contains contact cutouts where fingers occluded the plaque in the
supplied held pose. No missing contours, rear surfaces or new brand geometry are
invented. CSS framing and transforms do not change this asset's provenance.

The [signature source process](../SIGNATURE_ASSET_PROCESS.md) records the original
SVG image wrappers, exact pixel separation and remaining 3D/hand-rig limitations.
Homepage/footer source and choreography are preserved by this About-only task.

## Original editorial artwork

The modular story graphics and abstract process diagrams are new SVG/CSS design
studies for this page. They illustrate the supplied story and process; they are
not client deliverables, historical thesis pages, awards or fabricated project
proof. The typography, colours and existing canonical copy remain DrawnOut's.
No competitor graphics, stock visuals or new rendering package are introduced.

## Evidence

The capture tool records actual production-browser frames and native timestamped
video. H.264 versions preserve the untrimmed original timing. No application
animation is sought, paused or replaced for capture. Source hashes and viewport
sizes are recorded in the gallery's capture manifest. Cloud Chromium software
rendering is review evidence, not physical-device or Safari performance proof.
