# Source fidelity, approval and production limits

The new work is submitted for visual review. Passing validation does not confer
creative approval, establish project authorship or verify a client engagement.
All portfolio publication, withdrawal, factual-verification and credit controls
remain in effect. Supplied application imagery is shown as artwork, with no
invented results, services, testimonials or historical narrative.

## Project motion

The originals are flattened PNG presentation boards. Alpha mattes isolate real
visible pixels; they do not recover hidden artwork or turn raster images into
vectors. The 1440 × 810 landscape and 810 × 1080 portrait canvases provide a
high-resolution composition of appropriately sized pieces. Their dimensions do
not mean every logo or mockup has that much source detail.

## Portfolio sharpness

The source audit covers all 37 portfolio derivative assets and their approved
crops. Native full boards are typically 1554–1654px wide. Regeneration removes
unnecessary pre-downsampling and reduces compression loss; it cannot recover
information absent from the original PNG. Small detail panels are capped at
their native dimensions instead of being enlarged to fill an oversized frame.

High-DPR screens and full-width 1920px presentations still expose source limits.
For genuinely sharper imagery, provide high-resolution isolated final artwork
or editable project masters. No AI upscaling, invented detail or aggressive
sharpening is used. See [the complete image audit](IMAGE_QUALITY.md).

## Enquiry and browser evidence

The signature enquiry is integrated on Brand Identity & Design only. Its
fragments are conceptual labels, not submitted visitor information or a
simulated completed client project. The Contact CTA is ordinary semantic HTML
outside the animated SVG. The other four service enquiry sections are preserved
until this composition is approved.

The saved desktop/mobile enquiry recordings are real Chromium viewport
recordings with native timing, wheel input and no timeline seeking or injected
animation styles. Their manifest records six exact source-file hashes; these
were verified against the saved implementation. The cycle counter reaches two
after recording starts before navigation. Some initial desktop motion occurs
while scrolling into the section; the dedicated full-section static screenshots
show all copy and artwork together. Mobile recordings use Chromium viewport and
touch emulation, not physical phones.

Physical iOS Safari and Android verification remains outstanding. Headless
Chromium tests establish neither physical decoder/memory behavior nor Safari
rendering. Browser retrieval and automatic favicon selection can be verified;
they do not constitute a screenshot of actual browser-tab chrome.
