### Hero composition

Desktop:

Create an immersive, asymmetrical editorial hero.

The left portion should contain the eyebrow, oversized headline and an introductory paragraph.

The custom twin illustration occupies the right side or extends artfully into the lower center of the hero.

The faces must remain visible and never be cropped carelessly.

Use deliberate negative space around the text.

A fine orange line begins near the headline and visually connects the composition with the portrait.

Do not put the founders into separate cards.

The illustration and headline should visually belong to the same story.

The hero should have an intentional relationship with the site's header and its content below.

Mobile:

Stack the narrative carefully.

The headline remains bold and readable, the illustration becomes the primary visual, and the introductory copy follows in a comfortable reading width.

Preserve both faces without cutting off their features.

Do not make the user scroll through excessive empty space before encountering the founders.

The illustration should feel large and prominent, not reduced to a small thumbnail.

### Hero animation storyboard

Create one coordinated About-page entrance.

**0.0–0.5 seconds:**
Warm paper background appears. A fine orange line begins drawing across the composition.

**0.5–1.3 seconds:**
The headline reveals in two or three controlled editorial segments. Avoid an excessive letter-by-letter typing effect.

**0.8–2.7 seconds:**
The portrait begins revealing. If genuine vector paths exist, use an intentional stroke-drawing sequence. Otherwise, employ carefully designed masks revealing the existing illustrated artwork.

The drawing should progress through major contours and facial details, not randomly expose disconnected fragments.

**2.2–3.4 seconds:**
The paragraph and illustration caption appear. The founder portrait approaches its completed state.

**3.4–4.2 seconds:**
The composition settles. Orange accents become still or continue only with extremely subtle ambient movement.

The illustration remains visible as the actual page hero.

Timing is guidance, not permission to delay access to the content.

### About-specific loading behavior

This About hero is the page's arrival experience.

Do not place the existing full four-second DrawnOut Loop immediately before another four-second About animation.

Coordinate the route with the current global loader implementation. The homepage loader remains unchanged.

For `/about`, prefer a nonblocking progressive hero arrival. Content and navigation should be accessible immediately, and the design must work even if JavaScript fails.

Direct visits, internal navigation and refreshes should reveal the hero appropriately without a white flash, double animation, or hidden content.

Respect reduced-motion and Save Data settings.

**Draw. Reveal. Connect.**

## Drawing

## Revealing

Use for the headline, important typography and founder names.

## Connecting

Use for the story progression and creative-process visualization.

### Technical requirements

Avoid adding a large animation library unless there is a demonstrated need.

Prefer composited transforms, opacity, clipping and authentic SVG stroke drawing where appropriate.

- `prefers-reduced-motion`
- Save Data
- Browser visibility
- Keyboard navigation
- Native scrolling
- No-JavaScript fallback

After the initial reveal, any ambient motion must be very subtle.
